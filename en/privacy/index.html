Privacy

In short: without an account we know nothing about you. Everything you do in the game stays in your browser.

In your browser

Your profile, games, rating, lesson and puzzle progress and settings are kept in your browser’s local storage and are not sent to us. Erase them with “Reset progress” in your profile or by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Visit statistics

We count page views and game events (such as “game started” or “puzzle solved”) with GoatCounter: no cookies, no IP addresses in reports, no tracking across sites. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, we count nothing. Errors on pages are counted the same way: the error text only, no personal data.

An account — if you want one

If you sign in, we keep your email, nickname, rating, saved games, solved puzzles, daily-puzzle streak and language. They are stored with Supabase so that your progress is the same on all your devices. Other players may see your nickname and rating.

You can delete your account and all its data at any time with “Delete account” in your profile.

Playing by link

When you play a friend by link, the browsers connect directly (WebRTC). To find each other they use public Nostr relays and STUN servers run by Google and Cloudflare. Over a direct connection the opponent can technically learn your IP address. Only the players and spectators of the room see the moves.

Feedback

If you write to us through the form, we keep the message, language, page, browser details and your contact if you leave one, only to answer and to fix the problem.

Videos

YouTube videos load only when you press play, through youtube-nocookie.com. From then on YouTube’s own rules apply.

Supporting the project

Payments happen on the sites of the services you choose (such as Boosty or Ko-fi). We never see your card details and receive only what the service shows its creators.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising trackers or cookies.

Your rights

You can download your data at any time (“Download a copy” in your profile), delete it, or ask us anything through “Write to us”.

Changes

If these rules change, we will update the date at the bottom of this page.
