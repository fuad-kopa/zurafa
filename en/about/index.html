About Zurafa

Zurafa is a free game of Timur’s great chess, also called Tamerlane chess, and of other games of his age. Right in your browser, in six languages.

What is here

Great chess on an 11 × 10 board with two citadels: against the computer (six opponents from Recruit to Timur), with a friend by link, or two players at one screen. Lessons, puzzles and a daily puzzle, and eight of Timur’s battles on terrain maps.

The Garden of games: shatranj, oblong, complete and citadel chess, nard, toguz korgool and asyk atu. An encyclopedia with sources, and a cinema.

Our rules

Free, with no ads and nothing behind a paywall. We tell the history honestly: what is fact and what is tradition is marked, and every article lists its sources. Where the manuscripts disagree, the player chooses the rules.

Why “Zurafa”

Zurafa is Arabic for giraffe: a piece of great chess found in no other chess.

Who makes it

A small independent team. The pictures of the rooms and pieces were made with the help of neural networks and finished by hand; they are artistic reconstructions, not pictures of real objects. The Piazzolla and Ysabeau Office fonts are used under the SIL Open Font License. Rights to other people’s videos in the cinema belong to their authors.
