The garden of games

Games of Timur’s court and of the steppe around Samarkand. Each has an encyclopedia article: what is known for certain and what remains tradition.

  • ShatranjThe small 8 × 8 chess from which both Timur’s great chess and modern chess grew. The ferz and the alfil are weak, so games are slow and positional.
  • NardDice and counters. By Persian tradition the sage Bozorgmehr invented nard in reply to the chess sent from India.
  • Toguz korgoolA game of pits and stones known across the steppe from the Tian Shan to Kazakhstan: nine pits a side, captures by even counts.
  • Asyk atuAsyk atu: a heavy shooter bone, the saka, knocks knucklebones out of a ring. Sheep ankle bones for games turn up in excavations across Central Asia; Kazakh asyk games are on UNESCO’s heritage list.
