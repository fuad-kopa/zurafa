## Hacker News — Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: Zurafa – Tamerlane chess (11×10, giraffes, citadels) in the browser

**Text:**
I've been building Zurafa, a free browser version of Tamerlane chess (shatranj al-kabir), the large chess variant associated with Timur's court in the 14th century. It's played on an 11×10 board with two citadel squares sticking out of the sides, and each side has 28 pieces: giraffes, camels, siege engines, pickets, a vizier and general, and eleven pawns, each of which promotes to its own piece. The pawn of pawns promotes three times and can end up as a second king.

What's there:
- six computer opponents (alpha-beta with a transposition table, in a Web Worker; the first load is about 110 KB of gzipped JavaScript);
- play a friend by link (WebRTC, no account), or two people on one screen;
- a 200-lesson course, puzzles and a daily puzzle, eight of Timur's battles on terrain maps;
- seven more period games: shatranj, oblong chess with the die (described c. 850), complete and citadel chess, nard, toguz korgool, asyk;
- an encyclopedia of 29 articles with sources, in six languages.

The rules come from a single 15th-century Persian manuscript and are disputed in places, so the disputed points are options: you can play "by Murray", "by Forbes" or with shatranj-style endings.

Stack: TypeScript + Vite, no framework, SVG board, static pages generated for the encyclopedia. The room illustrations were made with image models and finished by hand.

I'd love feedback on the rules interface and on how the engine plays.

## Reddit — r/chessvariants

**Title:** I made a free browser version of Tamerlane chess, with the disputed rules as options

**Text:**
Hi all! I've been working on Zurafa, a free, ad-free site for Timur's great chess (Tamerlane chess, 11×10 + 2 citadels): https://zurafa.app/en/

A few things that might interest this sub:
- the disputed rules are switchable: Murray's reading, Forbes's (picket may step one square, pawn of pawns may fork the king), or shatranj-style endings with the bare king;
- the pawn of pawns works with all three stages, princes and the adventitious king included;
- you can also play oblong chess (4×16 with the die), complete chess 10×10 with king-stepping dabbabas and citadel chess from al-Amuli, based on Murray's ch. XVI;
- six AI levels, play by link, local two-player.

I'm especially interested in where you think my rule readings are wrong. There's a feedback form at the bottom of every page.
